# MISIA.SK APPS

Mobile apps of Misia (Slobodná ľudová misia), available for download at
**https://mirozahorak.github.io/misia-apps/**.

| App | What it is | Platforms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Misia.sk Reader](reader/)** | Christian books, circular letters and sermon transcripts, with offline reading, search, bookmarks and notes | iPhone/iPad (App Store), Android (Google Play closed testing) |
| **[Misia Akcie/Events](events/)** | Events, meetings and sermons — the next event for everyone; full event details, sermon recordings and live broadcasts after sign-in | Android, iPhone |
| **[Misia Spevník/Songbook](songbook/)** | A Christian songbook in six languages that works offline | Android, iPhone, iPad |
| **[Misia Biblická časová os](bible-timeline/)** | Bible and Christian history on one timeline, with scripture, also offline | Android |
| **[Misia Rádio](radio/)** | Misia's internet radio: sermons, songs and audiobooks live, with scripture, lyrics and the week's programme | Android |
| **[Misia Wordcast](wordcast/)** | Sermon search, offline listening, and Standard and Simple modes | [Android public test](https://github.com/mirozahorak/misia-apps/releases/download/wordcast-v1.0.1-1/misia-wordcast.apk), [iPhone/iPad (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6824054088) |
| **[DeliveryTracker](deliverytracker/)** | Equipment delivery records for churches (for authorised mission staff) | Android |

## Store availability

Reader, Songbook, Events and Misia Wordcast are publicly available on the Apple App Store. Their links were verified against Apple's Slovak public catalogue.

Radio and Reader have Google Play closed tests, not public production releases. New volunteers first join [Misia App Testers](https://groups.google.com/g/misia-app-testers) using their Play account, then opt in separately for each app. Radio group access is available; Reader group access is still awaiting approval. Existing invited Alpha testers can access both. These states come from the release tracker's 4 November Console evidence; recheck before changing availability labels.

This catalogue is for Misia apps; Builder for BuildTrack has a separate business audience.

## Language-specific links

Share `?lang=sk`, `?lang=cs` or `?lang=en` to open the catalogue or an app page in Slovak, Czech or English. A valid URL language overrides the visitor's saved preference. Language buttons update the current URL, preserving other query parameters and app anchors (for example `?lang=en#reader`). Unsupported values fall back to the saved preference, then Slovak.
