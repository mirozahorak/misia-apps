# MISIA.SK APPS

Apps of Misia (Slobodná ľudová misia), available for download at
**https://mirozahorak.github.io/misia-apps/**.

| App | What it is | Platforms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Misia.sk Reader](reader/)** | Christian books, circular letters and sermon transcripts, with offline reading, search, bookmarks and notes | iPhone/iPad (App Store), Android (Google Play test) |
| **[Misia Akcie/Events](events/)** | Events, meetings and sermons — the next event for everyone; full event details, sermon recordings and live broadcasts after sign-in | Android, iPhone |
| **[Misia Spevník/Songbook](songbook/)** | A Christian songbook in six languages that works offline | Android, iPhone, iPad |
| **[Misia Biblická časová os](bible-timeline/)** | Bible and Christian history on one timeline, with scripture, also offline | Android, iPhone, iPad |
| **[MyBible.rs](mybible/)** | Bible study on the Mac: translations side by side, fast search, notes and studies; works with MyBible modules and includes the King James Version | macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, notarized) |
| **[Misia Rádio](radio/)** | Misia's internet radio: sermons, songs and audiobooks live, with scripture, lyrics and the week's programme | Android |
| **[Misia Wordcast](wordcast/)** | Sermon search, offline listening, and Standard and Simple modes | Android (Google Play test or APK), iPhone/iPad (App Store) |
| **[DeliveryTracker](deliverytracker/)** | Equipment delivery records for churches (for authorised mission staff) | Android |
| **[Misia Audiobooks](audiobooks/)** | Christian audiobooks and circular letters | Web listening, mobile release in preparation |
| **[Misia Tape Identifier](tape-verifier/)** | Check whether a physical tape has already been digitized | Mobile release in preparation |
| **[Misia.sk Missions](mission/)** | Help requests, churches and receipts showing how the money was spent (for mission workers and church leaders) | Android |

## Store availability

Reader, Songbook, Events, Bible Timeline and Misia Wordcast are publicly available on the Apple App Store. Their links were verified against Apple's Slovak public catalogue.

Radio, Reader and Misia Wordcast have active Google Play tests. Choose **Get the Android app** on a detail page. Join [Misia App Testers](https://groups.google.com/g/misia-app-testers/about) once with the account you use in Google Play, then return to the app page to join that app’s test and install it. Apps with a test in preparation show when their Play link is pending.

This catalogue is for Misia apps; Builder for BuildTrack has a separate business audience.

## Language-specific links

Share `?lang=sk`, `?lang=cs` or `?lang=en` to open the catalogue or an app page in Slovak, Czech or English. A valid URL language overrides the visitor's saved preference. Language buttons update the current URL, preserving other query parameters and app anchors (for example `?lang=en#reader`). Unsupported values fall back to the saved preference, then Slovak.
