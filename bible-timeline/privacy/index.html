Privacy Policy — Misia Bible Timeline

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Misia Bible Timeline is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It presents biblical and Christian history through periods, people, events, Bible passages and pictures. You can read the bundled content without an account or an internet connection. The app contains no advertisements, usage analytics or advertising trackers.

Information stored on your device

Your language, Bible translation, display filters, text size, reading history and downloaded timeline data are stored on your device. The app does not upload your reading history, search text or display preferences. Removing the app removes its local data; your device backup settings may retain a backup.

Data updates and internet requests

The app checks for timeline updates from https://nasa.misia.sk over HTTPS after launch and when it returns to the foreground, subject to its update interval. You can also request a check in settings. Requests include a content language and, where available, a cache identifier. Download requests for detail texts identify the content and Bible translation. If the server is unavailable, the bundled content remains available.

Servers receive ordinary connection information, including an IP address and request details. Technical logs may be kept to deliver the service and maintain security. The app does not use these requests for advertising or analytics. Fonts are bundled in the app and do not require downloads from Google Fonts.

Sharing and external links

Sharing happens when you tap Share and choose a destination in your device's share sheet. The selected destination handles the link under its own privacy terms. External reference links and the online privacy policy open in a browser, where the website's own privacy terms apply.

Support and your choices

If you contact info@misia.sk by email, the details you choose to send are used to answer your enquiry. You can ask about access, correction or deletion of your correspondence at that address. The app itself has no sign-in or user accounts. You can read the bundled timeline offline and remove local app data through your device settings.

Permissions and children

The app uses internet access for data updates and external links. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo access. Reading the timeline requires no personal details. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information by email or through another service.
