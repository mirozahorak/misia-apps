Privacy Policy

Misia Akcie/Events — last updated 1 August 2026

The Misia Akcie/Events mobile application ("the App") is operated by Misia (misia.sk) to help participants of our events view event information, programs and sermons, and manage event registrations.

What data we collect

  • Account data: name and e-mail address, used to sign in and identify you within the App.
  • Event registration data: information you provide when registering for an event, such as names of registered participants, accommodation and meal preferences, and notes you enter.

Browsing the public part of the App — the upcoming event and church-service information shown on the home screen — requires no account and collects no personal data.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to organize our events — managing registrations, accommodation, meals and communication related to the events. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not profile you, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on servers operated by Misia. Communication between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@misia.sk. Deleting your account removes your personal data from our systems, except where we are legally required to retain it.

Children

Registrations may include information about children provided by their parent or guardian for the purpose of event organization (e.g. accommodation and meals).

Contact

Misia — www.misia.sk — info@misia.sk
