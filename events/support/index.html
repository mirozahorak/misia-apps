Support

Misia Akcie/Events — last updated 1 August 2026

The Misia Akcie/Events app lets participants of Misia events view event information and programs, listen to sermons, follow live broadcasts, and manage event registrations.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No — the upcoming event and church-service information is available to everyone. Sign in for complete event details and MP3 recordings of previous services.
  • How do I sign in? Use the e-mail address and password from your registration on nasa.misia.sk.
  • I forgot my password. Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen, or contact us.
  • How do I register for an event? Open the event in the App and tap Register, or register on nasa.misia.sk.
  • Why can I only view my registration? Registrations become read-only after the event's changes deadline has passed.
