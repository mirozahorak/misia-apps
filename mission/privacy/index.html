Privacy Policy

Misia.sk Missions — last updated 17 November 2026

The Misia.sk Missions mobile application ("the App") is operated by Misia (misia.sk) for mission workers and church leaders. It is used to submit help requests, keep church records and document how money sent by the mission was spent.

Who can use it

Only people with a misia.sk account and access to the mission section can sign in. What each person sees depends on the rights the mission gives them: their own requests, the requests of their region, or everything.

What data the App handles

  • Account data: your name, e-mail address and roles, used to sign you in and to show what you may see and do.
  • Help requests: what is needed, amounts, the church and, where relevant, the name of the person being helped, with comments and approvals.
  • Churches: church names, places and leaders' contact details that you or other mission workers enter.
  • Photos and receipts: pictures you take or choose for a request, a church or a receipt. They are uploaded to misia.sk, and receipts go to the mission's accounting.
  • Finance: gifts and expenses, shown only to people who have the right to see them.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on the misia.sk servers. The App keeps a copy on your phone so it works offline; changes made offline wait on the phone until they can be sent. Signing out removes this copy from the phone.

Camera, photos and microphone

The App asks for the camera and your photo library only when you add a photo or a receipt. The microphone permission exists only because the camera component may record video; the App does not record sound.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics and no selling or sharing of data with third parties. Data is used only for the mission's work and its accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to info@misia.sk. Records needed for accounting may have to be kept for the period the law requires.
