Misia.sk Missions support

Last updated 17 November 2026

Misia.sk Missions is for mission workers and church leaders: help requests, churches, and receipts showing how the money sent was used. It runs on Android 7.0 and later, in Slovak and English.

Getting access

Sign in with your misia.sk account. If the App says your account has no access, ask the mission administrator to give you access to the mission section. Settings → Your access shows what you can see and do, and which server the App uses.

After the money is sent

When a request is marked as paid, the App asks for receipts. Add a photo of each receipt, or of what was bought, and mark the request as documented when nothing more is coming.

Working offline

New requests, comments, receipts and photos are saved on the phone first and sent when you are back online. Settings → Outbox shows what is still waiting. Do not uninstall the App or sign out while the Outbox has items: they would be lost.

Installing an update

Download the new APK from the app page and install it over the old one; your data stays.

Contact

Contact your mission administrator first. For application support: info@misia.sk. Include your Android version, the App version (at the bottom of Settings) and a description of the problem. Never send your password.
