Privacy Policy — MyBible.rs

Last updated: 15 November 2026

MyBible.rs is a Bible study app for macOS, published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It reads Bible, dictionary and commentary modules in the MyBible format. The app has no sign-in or user accounts, and contains no advertisements, usage analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your Mac

Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, studies, reading positions, reading history and settings are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/MyBible.rs on your Mac. Bible and other modules are stored in the module folder you choose (by default ~/MyBible). The app does not upload this information. Backups you create are saved where you choose. Removing the app does not remove these folders; you can delete them yourself.

Internet requests

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- Browsing or refreshing the module catalogue downloads public module lists from MyBible module sources (for example mybible.zone) and from any source you add yourself. Installing a module downloads it from the address given in that list.
- Syncing Sermons & Books downloads a list of Misia sermons and books related to Bible passages from https://nasa.misia.sk. The list is saved on your Mac for offline use.
- Links to published sermons, books or websites open in your browser.

These servers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address and request details, and may keep technical logs to deliver the service and keep it secure. The app does not send your notes, searches or reading history in these requests. Third-party module sources handle requests under their own privacy terms.

Clipboard and sharing

Verses are copied to the clipboard only when you choose Copy or Export. Exported files are saved where you choose.

Permissions and children

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Reading and studying requires no personal details. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information by email.

Support and your choices

If you contact info@misia.sk, the details you choose to send are used to answer your enquiry. You can ask about access, correction or deletion of your correspondence at that address.

Slovenské zhrnutie

MyBible.rs je aplikácia na štúdium Biblie pre macOS bez účtu, reklamy a sledovania. Poznámky, zvýraznenia, štúdie, história čítania a nastavenia sú uložené len vo vašom Macu (~/Library/Application Support/MyBible.rs) a moduly v priečinku, ktorý si zvolíte (predvolene ~/MyBible). Aplikácia sa pripája na internet iba vtedy, keď o to požiadate: pri prehliadaní a sťahovaní modulov zo zdrojov MyBible a pri synchronizácii kázní a kníh zo servera nasa.misia.sk. Servery dostávajú bežné technické údaje vrátane IP adresy; vaše poznámky, hľadania ani históriu čítania aplikácia neodosiela. Ak nám napíšete na info@misia.sk, použijeme zaslané údaje na vybavenie otázky.
