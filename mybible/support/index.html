Support — MyBible.rs

For help, bug reports or module questions, email info@misia.sk.

Please include the app version (MyBible.rs › About MyBible.rs), your macOS version, whether your Mac has Apple Silicon or Intel, the translation you were reading and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Installing

Open the downloaded DMG and drag MyBible.rs to the Applications folder. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. On first launch it creates the module folder ~/MyBible and adds the King James Version. If you already have a MyBible folder with modules, the app uses it.

Adding translations

Choose Manage Modules… (⌘M) to browse and install Bibles, dictionaries and commentaries from public MyBible module sources. You can also copy MyBible module files (.SQLite3) into the module folder and choose Rescan Module Folder (⇧⌘R).

Your notes and backups

Notes, highlights, studies and settings are stored on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/MyBible.rs. Back Up Now (⇧⌘B) saves a checked copy in the backups folder next to them, and Settings › Restore from backup brings a backup back after showing you what it contains.
