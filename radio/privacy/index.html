Privacy Policy — Misia Radio

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Misia Radio is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It plays Misia's internet radio stations and shows the programme, scripture, song lyrics and books that go with them. You do not need an account. There are no advertisements, usage analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen station, saved items (sermons, scripture passages, songs and books), where you stopped in recordings, reminders, and your theme, language and text-size preferences are stored only on your device. A copy of recently viewed programme and content is kept on the device so it can be read without a connection. None of this is uploaded to Misia. Removing the app removes its local data; your device's backup settings may retain a backup.

What the app downloads

To play and describe the radio, the app requests station status, the programme, and sermon, song and book details from https://nasa.misia.sk over HTTPS. It plays the station's audio stream and sermon recordings from Misia's media servers. These requests contain only what is needed to answer them, such as the station, the date or the recording. They do not include your saved items or reminders.

Like any internet request, the servers that answer receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, and may keep it briefly in technical logs to deliver the service and keep it secure.

Reminders and notifications

If you ask to be reminded before a programme starts, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system; nothing about them is sent to Misia. While audio plays, the app shows a playback notification with the current title and controls.

Sharing and links

Sharing only happens when you tap Share, through your device's share sheet. Links to books, sermons and the Misia website open in your browser, where that website's own privacy terms apply.

Permissions and children

On Android the app uses network access, background audio playback and, only if you set a reminder, notifications. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Listening does not require any personal details, so the app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

The app does not collect personal information. If you write to us through the support page or by email, see the support page for how messages are handled. Questions: info@misia.sk.

The current policy is available at Misia Radio privacy policy. Changes will be published here with an updated date.

Slovenské zhrnutie

Misia Radio prehráva internetové rádio Misie bez prihlásenia, bez reklamy a bez analytického sledovania. Zvolená stanica, uložené položky, miesto, kde ste skončili v nahrávke, pripomienky a nastavenia zostávajú len vo vašom zariadení. Aplikácia sťahuje stav stanice, program a údaje o kázňach, piesňach a knihách zo servera nasa.misia.sk cez HTTPS a prehráva zvuk z mediálnych serverov Misie; tieto servery vidia bežné technické údaje ako IP adresu. Pripomienky plánuje operačný systém vo vašom zariadení a nič sa o nich neodosiela.
