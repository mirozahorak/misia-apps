Privacy Policy — Misia Radio

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Misia Radio is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It plays Misia's internet radio stations and shows the programme, scripture, song lyrics and books that go with them. You do not need an account. There are no advertisements or advertising trackers in the app. The app does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen station, saved items (sermons, scripture passages, songs and books), where you stopped in recordings, reminders, and your theme, language and text-size preferences are stored only on your device. A copy of recently viewed programme and content is kept on the device so it can be read without a connection. None of this is uploaded to Misia. Removing the app removes its local data; your device's backup settings may retain a backup.

What the app downloads

To play and describe the radio, the app requests station status, the programme, and sermon, song and book details from https://nasa.misia.sk over HTTPS. It plays the station's audio stream and sermon recordings from Misia's media servers. These requests contain only what is needed to answer them, such as the station, the date or the recording. They do not include your saved items or reminders.

Misia’s API and audio servers receive your IP address and ordinary HTTP request information, including the resource requested and request time. Technical request logs are retained for one week and then deleted. They are used to operate and secure the service, not for advertising or building listener profiles. Hosting providers may process these requests on Misia’s behalf. Your saved items, reminders and locally stored listening history are not uploaded.

Reminders and notifications

If you ask to be reminded before a programme starts, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system; nothing about them is sent to Misia. While audio plays, the app shows a playback notification with the current title and controls.

Sharing and links

Sharing only happens when you tap Share, through your device's share sheet. Links to books, sermons and the Misia website open in your browser, where that website's own privacy terms apply.

Permissions and children

On Android the app uses network access, background audio playback and, only if you set a reminder, notifications. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. You do not need to provide a name, email address, phone number or account profile to listen.

Contact

If you write to us through the support page or by email, see the support page for how messages are handled. Questions: info@misia.sk.

The current policy is available at Misia Radio privacy policy. Changes will be published here with an updated date.

Slovenské zhrnutie

Misia Radio prehráva internetové rádio Misie bez prihlásenia, bez reklamy a bez reklamných sledovacích nástrojov. Zvolená stanica, uložené položky, miesto, kde ste skončili v nahrávke, pripomienky a nastavenia zostávajú len vo vašom zariadení. Aplikácia sťahuje stav stanice, program a údaje o kázňach, piesňach a knihách zo servera nasa.misia.sk cez HTTPS a prehráva zvuk z mediálnych serverov Misie; tieto servery prijímajú bežné technické údaje vrátane IP adresy a informácií o HTTP požiadavke. Technické záznamy požiadaviek sa uchovávajú jeden týždeň a potom sa vymažú. Slúžia na prevádzku a bezpečnosť služby, nie na reklamu ani vytváranie profilov poslucháčov. Aplikácia je určená dospelým od 18 rokov. Pripomienky plánuje operačný systém vo vašom zariadení a nič sa o nich neodosiela.
