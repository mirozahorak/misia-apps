Support

Misia Radio — last updated 23 October 2026

Misia Radio plays Misia's internet radio — sermons, songs and audiobooks, live and in your language — with the scripture, lyrics and books that go with them.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I change the station? Tap the station name at the top of the Listen screen, or open More › Station.
  • The sound stopped. The app reconnects by itself when the connection drops and always returns to the live broadcast. If a station is not broadcasting, the Listen screen says so.
  • Can I hear a sermon from the beginning? Yes. Choose “Listen from the beginning”, or open the sermon and tap any scripture passage to hear that part.
  • Where does the scripture come from? Passages are marked in each sermon and shown as they are read. Open a sermon to choose another Bible translation.
  • How do reminders work? Tap the bell next to a sermon or chapter in the programme. You will be notified a few minutes before it starts.
  • Can the radio stop by itself? Yes — use the sleep timer (the moon icon), including “at the end of this sermon”.

Installing the Android app

Download the APK from MISIA.SK APPS. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from your browser — this is normal for apps distributed outside Google Play.
