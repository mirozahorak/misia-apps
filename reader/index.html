Misia.sk Reader

Christian books, circular letters and sermon transcripts from Misia.sk.

Read the included publications offline. Search for a passage, save bookmarks and notes, and return to your last chapter. Choose your content languages and adjust the reading page.

No account. No ads. Bookmarks and reading history stay on your device.

Available for iPhone and iPad: Download on the App Store.

Android is in closed testing on Google Play; the public release is being prepared. First join the tester group with the Google account you use in Google Play, then open the testing link and opt in.

As of 4 November 2026, access for new group members is awaiting Google approval. Existing invited testers can already use the testing link.
