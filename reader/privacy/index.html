Privacy policy — Misia.sk Reader

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Misia.sk Reader is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It contains Christian books, circular letters and sermon transcripts. No account is needed. The app has no advertising, advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data on your device

Bookmarks, folders, notes, reading history, saved reading positions and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not upload them to Misia or sync them between devices. Your operating system may include them in device backups. Removing the app removes its local data; backups are managed separately in your system settings.

Network requests and server logs

Publication text and fonts are included with the app. Cover images are downloaded over HTTPS from www.misia.sk. If you open a PDF, EPUB or other external link, the destination opens in another app and its privacy practices apply.

Misia's cover and download servers receive your IP address and ordinary HTTP request information, including the requested resource and request time. Basic technical access logs are kept for one week and then deleted. They are used to operate and secure the service, not for advertising or building reader profiles. Hosting providers may process requests on Misia's behalf.

Clipboard, sharing and permissions

The selection bookmark action reads clipboard text only after you request that action. Sharing uses your device's share sheet and the destination you choose. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Audience and contact

The app is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. It does not require a name, email address, phone number or profile to read. If you email support, we use your message and reply address to respond. Do not include private notes or reading history unless you want to share them.

For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, email info@misia.sk. Server logs are deleted after one week; locally stored app data stays under your control.

GitHub hosts this page; visits to the website are subject to GitHub's privacy statement. This policy will be updated here when our practices change.
