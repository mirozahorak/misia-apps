Help with Misia.sk Reader

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Read Christian books, circular letters and sermon transcripts from Misia.sk on your phone or tablet.

Start reading

Choose your interface and content languages during setup. Let the included library finish installing, then open a publication and tap Read book. Continue takes you back to your saved chapter.

Read without internet

Publication text is included with the app. Cover images and external PDF or EPUB downloads may need a connection.

Find a passage

Open search from the Library. Search for several words or choose Exact Phrase. Tap a result to open its chapter.

Keep your place

Bookmarks, notes, reading history and saved positions stay on your device. They do not sync between devices. Avoid uninstalling the app if you need to keep them.

Report a problem

Email info@misia.sk. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, content language, publication title and chapter. Tell us what you expected and what happened. Only attach screenshots or private notes if you want to share them.

We use your email address and message to answer your request. You can ask us to delete support correspondence at the same address.
