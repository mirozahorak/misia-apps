Privacy Policy

Misia Songbook — last updated 1 August 2026

The Misia Songbook mobile application ("the App") is published by Misia (misia.sk). It is an offline hymn songbook.

Short version

The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • The hymn library itself, which is bundled with the App and works without an internet connection.
  • Your preferences: saved (favourite) songs, chosen language, sort order, font and text size.

This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Data you choose to send

The App contains a Contact form. If — and only if — you use it, the name, e-mail address and message you type are sent to Misia over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) so that we can reply. We use them for nothing else.

Permissions

The App requests only internet access, which is used for the Contact form. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The App contains hymn lyrics only and is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

If you have sent us a message and want it deleted, write to info@misia.sk.

Contact

Misia — www.misia.sk — info@misia.sk
