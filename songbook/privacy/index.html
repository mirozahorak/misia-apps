Privacy Policy — Misia Songbook

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Misia Songbook is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. You can browse and read the bundled song library without an account or an internet connection. There are no advertisements, usage analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Information stored on your device

The song database, downloaded song updates, favourites, language, sorting, font, theme and text-size preferences are stored on your device. Favourites and preferences are not uploaded to Misia. Removing the app removes its local app data; your device's backup settings may retain a backup.

Song updates and fonts

When an internet connection is available, the app requests the language catalogue and song updates from https://nasa.misia.sk over HTTPS. Languages without server content continue to use locally stored songs. These requests include the requested language and, when available, a cache identifier for the previous song snapshot. They do not send favourites or search text.

The app uses Google Fonts and may download and cache font files from Google's font servers. Those servers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Font downloads are not advertising or analytics integrations. See Google's privacy policy.

Messages you choose to send

If you use Contact in the app or the Songbook support website, your name, email address, subject and message are sent over HTTPS to nasa.misia.sk and forwarded to Misia's support mailbox at info@misia.sk. We use these details to answer your enquiry, correct song information and provide support. Do not include sensitive personal information in a message.

Messages are processed by Misia's server, its background mail queue and email service. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle your enquiry and related follow-up. You can request deletion by emailing info@misia.sk; applicable legal obligations and existing backups may limit immediate deletion. We do not sell your personal information or use support messages for advertising.

Website hosting and technical logs

The privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Google's font servers and Misia's servers may process IP addresses and technical request information to deliver their services and maintain security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions and children

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Reading songs does not require personal details. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending personal information through Contact.

Your choices and contact

You can read local songs offline and choose whether to send a message. For access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to us, email info@misia.sk.

The current policy is available at Songbook privacy policy. For help, visit Songbook support. Changes will be published here with an updated date.

Slovenské zhrnutie

Misia Songbook umožňuje čítať uložené piesne bez prihlásenia a bez internetu. Obľúbené piesne a nastavenia zostávajú v zariadení. Pri pripojení aplikácia sťahuje aktualizácie piesní zo servera nasa.misia.sk a môže sťahovať písma zo služby Google Fonts. Ak použijete Kontakt, meno, e-mail, predmet a správa sa odošlú Misii na vybavenie vašej otázky. Webové stránky sú na GitHub Pages; prevádzkovatelia serverov môžu spracúvať IP adresu a technické údaje požiadaviek. Aplikácia neobsahuje reklamu ani analytické sledovanie. O prístup, opravu alebo vymazanie odoslaných údajov môžete požiadať na info@misia.sk.
