Privacy Policy — Misia Tape Verifier

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Misia Tape Verifier is published by Miroslav Zahorak for Misia. It checks whether sermon cassette tapes produced by Freie Volksmission Krefeld have already been digitized and are available on misia.sk. It needs no account. There are no advertisements, usage analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Camera

The app uses the camera to read the date written on a cassette label. Camera images are processed on your device by Apple's text recognition and are not stored or uploaded. The only exception is optional AI reading, described below, which is off by default.

Information stored on your device

The sermon catalogue, your scan history (label text, date, result and recording language), your settings, and the name and email you entered for reports are stored on your device. Removing the app removes this data; your device's backup settings may retain a backup.

Catalogue updates and recordings

When an internet connection is available, the app requests catalogue updates from https://nasa.misia.sk over HTTPS. When you play or download a recording, open a transcript or a YouTube video, the file or page is requested from www.misia.sk, nasa.misia.sk or YouTube. These requests do not include your scan history, name or email.

Reports you choose to send

If you tap Send report, your name, email address, an optional note and a list of the tapes you scanned since your last report (label dates and times, places, recording language and whether each recording is already digitized) are sent over HTTPS to nasa.misia.sk. The report is stored in Misia's support inbox and emailed to the archive team. We use it to find recordings that are missing from the archive and to contact you about sending tapes. Do not include sensitive personal information in the note.

Reports are kept as long as needed to complete the archive and handle related correspondence. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing info@misia.sk; legal obligations and existing backups may limit immediate deletion. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Optional AI reading

In Settings you can enable AI reading for labels the camera cannot read. It is off by default. When you enable it, a cropped photo of the label is sent to the provider you choose (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, or your own local server), using an API key you enter yourself. That provider processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy. API keys are stored in your device's keychain and are not sent to Misia.

Website hosting and technical logs

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and Misia's servers may process IP addresses and technical request information to deliver their services and keep them secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending a report.

Contact

Email info@misia.sk. Postal address: Slobodná ľudová misia, Ivana Krasku 18, 917 05 Trnava, Slovakia.

Slovenské zhrnutie

Misia Tape Verifier overuje, či sú kazety Freie Volksmission Krefeld už digitalizované. Fotoaparát číta dátum na štítku priamo v zariadení; obrázky sa neukladajú ani neodosielajú, okrem voliteľného čítania pomocou AI, ktoré je predvolene vypnuté a posiela orezaný obrázok štítku vami zvolenému poskytovateľovi. Ak odošlete hlásenie, meno, e-mail, poznámka a zoznam naskenovaných kaziet sa odošlú Misii, aby sme mohli doplniť archív a kontaktovať vás. Aplikácia neobsahuje reklamu ani analytické sledovanie. O prístup, opravu alebo vymazanie údajov môžete požiadať na info@misia.sk.
