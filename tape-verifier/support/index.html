Misia Tape Verifier — last updated 26 October 2026

Misia Tape Verifier checks whether a sermon cassette produced by Freie Volksmission Krefeld over the last 50 years has already been digitized and is available in your language on misia.sk and in the Misia apps.

Frequently asked questions

  • What do the colours mean? Green: the recording is already digitized. Red: it is not digitized yet — please keep the tape. Yellow: check the label, or choose the right meeting when several match.
  • The label is not recognised. Hold the label steady inside the frame and tap it on the screen to focus. Use the torch in a dark room, or tap “Correct label” and type the date.
  • Can I put the phone on a table? Yes. Switch to the front camera and hold each tape above the phone.
  • Which language is checked? The recording language shown at the top of the scan screen. Change it there.
  • What happens when I send a report? Your list of scanned tapes, name and email go to the Misia archive team. If some tapes need digitizing, the app shows where to send them.
